# CPT Tournament Bracket

**8 teams · 4 robots per match · 12 rounds · one ring**

---

## Timing

5 min per slot (4 min battle + 1 min turnover) × 12 rounds = **60 min** scheduled.  
With intro and margin for slow resets: ~65 min — fits within a 75-min period.

## Seeding note

R1 and R2 are the two same-pool rounds. If you have a sense of team strength before the tournament, put your two suspected strongest teams into different pools (one into 1–2–3–4, one into 5–7–9–10) — they won't face each other until at least R7.

## Back-to-back note

With one ring, some teams will have consecutive rounds (e.g. teams 5 and 7 play both R2 and R3). For robots this is just a quick re-placement. Teams that want to SSH and adjust code between rounds should plan ahead — they'll have roughly 5 minutes during the preceding round.
